# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name

LumaBloom – Ambient Glow

## Summary

Cinematic, video-synced ambient lighting for YouTube™ — private, adjustable, and processed locally on your device.

## Detailed description

Let every frame light the room.

LumaBloom extends the colors of the video you're watching into a smooth ambient glow across the page. The effect responds to the edges of each frame to make movies, music videos, and streams feel more immersive.

Features:

- Video-synced color zones for a rich, full-page glow
- Adjustable intensity, spread, color boost, response, and smoothness
- Works in normal and theater view
- Lightweight local processing
- No accounts, analytics, ads, or frame uploads

Privacy by design

LumaBloom processes video colors entirely inside your browser. Your video frames and sampled colors never leave your device. The extension requests only the storage permission, which saves your visual preferences locally.

Notes

- Designed for standard YouTube™ watch pages
- The effect rests in fullscreen and hidden tabs
- Protected video or browser cross-origin restrictions may limit color sampling

LumaBloom is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC.
